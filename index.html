Biography

Ruiyang Ren is a final-year Ph.D. candidate at Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. Wayne Xin Zhao. He received his M.E. from Renmin University of China in 2022, supervised by Prof. Ji-Rong Wen and Prof. Wayne Xin Zhao, and his B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from Taishan College (honor college), Shandong University in 2019, supervised by Prof. Xuemeng Song and Prof. Liqiang Nie. His research interests include information retrieval and large language models.
