Biography

Ruiyang Ren is a 3rd-year Ph.D. student in Artificial intelligence at Gaoling School of Artificial Intellegence, Renmin University of China (RUC). His supervisor is Prof. Wayne Xin Zhao. He completed his M.E. at RUC (2022), supervised by Prof. Ji-Rong Wen and Wayne Xin Zhao, and B.E. of Computer Science and Technology at Taishan College, Shandong University (2019) supervised by Prof. Xuemeng Song and Liqiang Nie. His current research interests include information retrieval and large language models.
